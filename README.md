# CoreView for IP Cameras

**Watch your own IP cameras live on Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro. ONVIF, RTSP, UniFi Protect and Rhombus in one native app, straight over your own network.**

You've got a doorbell from one brand, a driveway camera from another, and a UniFi Protect system in the garage, so you end up with three apps and none of them sits nicely on your Mac. CoreView puts them all in one live view. On the Mac, each camera or group gets its own window that you can place on any monitor.

## What it does

- **Adds cameras in seconds:** ONVIF discovery, RTSP network scanning, UniFi Protect console discovery (API key or local account), Rhombus, or paste any RTSP/RTSPS URL. Built-in, USB and Continuity cameras work too.
- **Camera groups as a live wall:** automatic grid, or arrange and resize cameras yourself. H.264, HEVC and AV1.
- **Made for the Mac:** favorite cameras in the menu bar; any camera or group in its own window, which you can put on any display. One customer runs CoreView across two large monitors and a third screen, with each camera window snapped into place by Rectangle Pro.
- **Recovers on its own:** streams only the cameras on screen, and a dropped stream comes back in about a second.
- **Snapshots and clips** straight to Photos, without re-encoding. Your existing recorder keeps doing continuous recording.
- **Apple TV:** turn any TV into a camera display, controlled with the Siri Remote.
- **Private:** video travels directly from your cameras to your device. No cloud service, and CoreView doesn't track what you watch.

## FAQ

**Is there a native Mac app for UniFi Protect?** Yes. CoreView connects to your Protect console directly over your network and runs natively on the Mac.

**Why does the Protect app on my Mac keep dropping streams?** On a Mac, Protect runs as the iPad app. Update it, allow Local Network access, and turn off VPNs or private DNS while testing. If you want a viewer that stays up on its own, CoreView reconnects a dropped stream in about a second.

**Does it replace my NVR?** No. Keep your recorder for recording; CoreView is the live view.

**Which cameras work?** Anything with ONVIF or an RTSP stream (Reolink, Amcrest, TP-Link Tapo, Lorex, Annke, EZVIZ, Axis and many more), plus UniFi Protect and Rhombus. Cloud-only cameras without ONVIF or RTSP may not connect.
